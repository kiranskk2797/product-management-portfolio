# Fireflies.ai Commitments — From meeting commitments to honest closure

**Kiran Kumar S · Independent product management capstone**

A meeting ends with “I’ll follow up.” What happens if the person named in the recap understood the conversation differently? This capstone explores how a team could confirm ownership and record what happened to a commitment inside Fireflies Tasks.

[Capstone presentation](./presentation.pdf)

## Product decision

The proposed workflow gives the suggested owner a chance to confirm or decline a commitment. A handoff counts as resolved only when the next owner accepts it. Completing the work and deliberately dropping it are also explicit resolutions. Silence, a blocker, or a pending handoff leaves the commitment open.

## How I would measure it

The proposed metric is the share of eligible commitments explicitly resolved within 14 days. The assignment supplied a 28% baseline and a six-month target of 45%. Whether the workflow improves that rate still needs testing. I would also check whether people felt pressured to accept ownership and whether reminders became burdensome.
